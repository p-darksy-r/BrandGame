A LITTLE RECOGNITION GOES A LONG WAY

How to play

  1. Name the brand

    Choose a category and a level. Type the name behind each logo, then press Enter.

  2. Need a nudge?

    A hint costs 5 points; a letter costs 10. You have three lives. Pause anytime with Esc.

  3. Build your collection

    Complete a level with 75% correct for one star, 90% for two, or a perfect score for three. Replays improve your records.
